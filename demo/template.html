One question, four ways to type it in Hindi

We asked seven open language models the same 296 factual questions in English, in Devanagari, in a rule-based romanization, and in Hinglish as a native speaker actually types it. Every model knew more when asked in Hinglish than in Devanagari.

English
When did George Orwell die?

Natural Hinglish
George Orwell kab mare the?

How it was measured

Each question was asked with three instruction wordings, written in the same form as the question. Decoding was greedy. An answer counts as right if it names the gold answer in any script; a model "gets" a question when it is right for at least two of the three wordings.

Checking the scorer

The automatic scorer was compared with 331 blind human judgments across all four forms: 97.6% agreement, Cohen's κ = 0.95. It almost never accepts a wrong answer (1 of 171) and occasionally misses a correct one (7 of 160).
